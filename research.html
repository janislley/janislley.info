RESEARCH AND AREAS OF EXPERTISE

Research

My technical work explores embedded systems and the interaction between software and hardware in consumer electronics.

Research interests

  • Embedded Linux systems
  • Linux kernel and device drivers
  • Battery and charging technologies
  • Wired and wireless power
  • Power management
  • Consumer electronics

My work includes Linux and Android development, driver bring-up, hardware integration, and charging and power-consumption features for mobile devices.

INDUSTRY AND APPLIED RESEARCH

Applied engineering

At SIDIA — Samsung, I contribute to embedded software development for smartphones and tablets, including device drivers and power-management features across multiple hardware platforms. My work spans product development, integration, testing, launch, and post-release support.

I have also contributed to more than 150 consumer-electronics device projects and authored more than 30 scientific publications.
