About me

I am Janislley Oliveira, an R&D Technical Leader and Senior Embedded Software Engineer at SIDIA — Samsung. I work on consumer electronics development for Latin America and have more than ten years of experience in embedded systems.

My work focuses on Linux and Android systems close to the hardware, including the Linux kernel, device drivers, power management, batteries, and wired and wireless charging technologies. Throughout my career, I have contributed to the development of more than 150 Samsung device projects.

I have also authored more than 30 scientific publications. I enjoy sharing knowledge, mentoring early-career professionals, and helping people connect with engineering.
